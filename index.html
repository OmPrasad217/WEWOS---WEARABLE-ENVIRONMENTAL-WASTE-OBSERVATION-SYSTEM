🔎 Inspect Integrated Modules

The supporting-sensor assembly conceptually contains sensing elements and integrated electronics. Only the compact external sensor housing is visible on the helmet; internal modules remain concealed.

📍 GPS / Location
Associates waste observations with geographical coordinates and time so detections can appear on the Waste Hotspot Map and Drain Risk Hotspot Map.
💾 Data Storage
Saves waste type, date, time, location, estimated quantity, drain-risk assessment, and historical observations for re-audit.
📏 Distance Sensing
Provides distance and proximity information that can support assessment near drainage areas.
🌡️ Environmental Sensing
Can provide ambient data, such as temperature and humidity, if appropriate sensing elements are installed.
⚙️ Processing Interface
Sends camera and sensor observations to the internal processing unit, where waste records, maps, and conceptual risk levels are created.
📡 Communication
Supports transmitting saved observation records to a dashboard or municipal reporting workflow when communication is available.
🔋 Power Regulation
Receives regulated power from the concealed internal battery system and distributes it to sensing and computing electronics.

Conceptual integration only: GPS, storage, communication, processing, and power electronics are not themselves environmental sensors. Their placement, wiring, antenna position, heat management, and performance require engineering validation.
